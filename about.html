About Us

Built by practitioners.
Not theorists.

A decade of solving real analytics problems inside growing businesses — before Fortify existed.

Fortify Technologies was created to solve a problem that most analytics agencies don't acknowledge: most data projects fail not because of bad technology, but because they start in the wrong place.

We've spent a decade embedded inside SaaS companies and e-commerce brands — not as consultants visiting for a few weeks, but as the people responsible for the numbers. We've felt the pressure of a leadership team that doesn't trust the dashboard. We've lived through the quarterly planning cycle where no one can agree on a single metric. We've watched good companies make bad decisions because their data told them a half-truth.

Fortify is the firm we wished existed when we were on the inside.

What makes us different

We are not a generalist analytics agency. We work exclusively with mid-market SaaS and e-commerce businesses at the growth inflection point — companies large enough to have data, agile enough to act on it.

Every engagement is senior-led. You work directly with the people who designed the methodology — not a project manager and a team of junior analysts. And every deliverable is built for handover: full documentation, training, and independence from day one.

Our conviction

Data is not the advantage. Knowing what to do with it is. The companies that win are not the ones with the most data — they're the ones who have built the clearest picture of their customer, their revenue, and their market, and who make decisions from that picture every single day.

That is what Fortify builds.
