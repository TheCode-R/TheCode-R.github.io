AI Business Dashboards

AI-narrated dashboards that explain why metrics moved and flag what needs attention — not just visualise what already happened.

The Problem

Most organisations have dashboards. Almost none have dashboards that get used every day. The gap between a dashboard that exists and a dashboard that changes decisions is the gap between data visualisation and data intelligence. We build the latter — one authoritative view of the business that your leadership team opens every morning because it tells them something they could not get anywhere else.

How AI changes this

The next generation of business dashboards does not just show data — it interprets it. Our AI layer runs on top of your dashboard to generate plain-language narrative summaries, flag anomalies automatically, and surface the signals your leadership team needs to act on — without spending 20 minutes reading charts.

What we deliver

  • Single source of truth across revenue, operations, and marketing
  • AI anomaly detection that surfaces unusual patterns automatically
  • AI-generated narrative summaries explaining what changed and why
  • Built in Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, or Metabase
