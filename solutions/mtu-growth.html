AI MTU Growth Intelligence

AI-powered Acquisition, Retention, and Reactivation framework built around Monthly Transacting Users — the one metric that shows whether your business is truly growing.

The Problem

Vanity metrics hide the truth. A SaaS company can show growing MAU while MTU is flat — because engaged-but-not-paying users mask the real conversion problem. An e-commerce brand can show strong new customer acquisition while repeat purchase rate is collapsing. MTU, tracked properly with AI-powered cohort modelling, shows the truth that aggregate metrics hide.

How AI changes this

Monthly Active Users tells you who logged in. Monthly Transacting Users tells you who gave you money. The difference between those two numbers contains your entire growth problem. Our AI models build the full picture — cohort by cohort, channel by channel, behaviour by behaviour — and forecast where MTU is headed under different scenarios.

What we deliver

  • Cohort-level acquisition analysis by channel, campaign, and segment
  • AI-identified retention drop-off points across the customer journey
  • Win-back model to reactivate dormant high-value customers at scale
  • AI MTU forecasting model for growth planning and scenario analysis
